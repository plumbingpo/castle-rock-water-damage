Warning Signs for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
